Frequently asked questions

Is the audio trimmer free?

Yes. The audio trimmer is 100% free with no limits, no watermark and no paid tier.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no account, sign-up or email required. Open the page and start using it.

How long should a ringtone be?

Most phones work well with 20 to 30 seconds. iPhone ringtones are limited to about 30 seconds.

MP3 or WAV: which should I choose?

MP3 is much smaller and works almost everywhere. WAV is lossless and larger, which is useful for further editing.

Can I use any song?

Only use audio you own or have the rights to use. This tool works with files already on your device and never downloads from other sites.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.
