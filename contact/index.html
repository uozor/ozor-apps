Contact

Questions, feedback, tool ideas or bug reports: we read every message.

Email us

The best way to reach GWB is by email: gwm.tech@greaterworldmedia.tech. We aim to reply within a few business days.

What to include

  • Bug reports: the tool name, your browser and device, and what happened.
  • Tool ideas: what you are trying to get done and how you do it today.
  • Reviews: tell us which tool you used and how it helped. With your permission, we may publish it on our home page.
  • Privacy requests: see our Privacy Policy.
