Guide

How to use it

Choose a video file from your device, pick a format and quality, and export. Your browser decodes the audio locally. Very long videos need plenty of memory. Use only videos you own or have the rights to.

Steps

  1. Open a video file from your device (MP4, WebM or MOV).
  2. Choose MP3 or WAV and a quality.
  3. Export and download the audio file.

Frequently asked questions

Is the video to MP3 extractor free?

Yes. The video to MP3 extractor is 100% free with no limits, no watermark and no paid tier.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no account, sign-up or email required. Open the page and start using it.

Which video formats work?

Any format your browser can decode. MP4 (AAC audio) and WebM work in most browsers; some MOV or HEVC files may not.

Can I extract audio from YouTube links?

No. This tool only works with video files already on your device that you own or have the rights to use.

What MP3 quality should I pick?

192 kbps is a good balance. Use 320 kbps for music where quality matters, or 128 kbps for speech.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.

Private by design: everything runs in your browser. Your files and text never leave your device.
