    Guide

    How to use it

    Enter one or two keywords that describe your business, pick a style, and generate fresh ideas. Click Generate again for more. Always check trademark and domain availability before choosing.

    Steps

    1. Enter one or two keywords that describe your business.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the business name generator free?

    Yes. The business name generator is 100% free with no limits, no watermark and no paid tier.

    Do I need an account or sign-up?

    No. There is no account, sign-up or email required. Open the page and start using it.

    Can I export or download my results?

    Yes. Use Save results in the Free forever box to download your inputs and results as PDF, Excel (.xlsx) or CSV, or tap Send to phone to share them as a plain-text note (share sheet, copy, .txt file or QR code). Files are created in your browser and include the date generated.

    Can I use any name I find here?

    Names are suggestions only. Check trademark databases, your local business registry and domain availability before using one.

    How do I choose a good business name?

    Keep it short, easy to spell and say, and relevant to what you offer. Say it out loud and check it on social platforms.

    Why do I get different names each time?

    The generator combines your keywords with style-based words randomly, so each click gives fresh ideas.

    Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

    No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.

    Private by design: everything runs in your browser. Your files and text never leave your device.
