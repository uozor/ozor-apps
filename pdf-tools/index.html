Guide

How to use it

Pick a task, choose your files, and download the result. Files are processed on your device with the open-source pdf-lib library. Password-protected PDFs may not work.

Steps

  1. Choose a task: merge, extract/split, or images to PDF.
  2. Select your files (PDFs or images).
  3. Click the button and download the result.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PDF merge & split tool free?

Yes. The PDF merge & split tool is 100% free with no limits, no watermark and no paid tier.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no account, sign-up or email required. Open the page and start using it.

Is there a file size or page limit?

There is no fixed limit. Very large files depend on your device's memory, since everything runs locally.

How do I extract specific pages?

Choose “Extract or split pages”, then type pages like “1-3, 5”. Tick “Save each page” to get one PDF per page.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Encrypted PDFs may fail or produce blank pages. Remove the password in your PDF reader first if you own the document.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.

Private by design: everything runs in your browser. Your files and text never leave your device.
