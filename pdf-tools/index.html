Frequently asked questions

Is there a file size or page limit?

There is no fixed limit. Very large files depend on your device's memory, since everything runs locally.

How do I extract specific pages?

Choose “Extract or split pages”, then type pages like “1-3, 5”. Tick “Save each page” to get one PDF per page.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Encrypted PDFs may fail or produce blank pages. Remove the password in your PDF reader first if you own the document.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.
