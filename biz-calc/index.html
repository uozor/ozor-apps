What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is profit as a percentage of the selling price. Markup is profit as a percentage of cost. A 50% markup equals a 33.3% margin.

How is the loan payment calculated?

It uses the standard amortization formula with monthly compounding: payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n).

What does break-even mean?

It is the number of units you must sell so that revenue covers fixed costs plus variable costs, with zero profit.
