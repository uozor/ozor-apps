Frequently asked questions

Is the policy generator free?

Yes. The policy generator is 100% free with no limits, no watermark and no paid tier.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no account, sign-up or email required. Open the page and start using it.

Are these policies legally binding?

They are general templates to help you get started, not legal advice. Laws vary by country and industry, so have a qualified lawyer review them.

Do I need a privacy policy?

Most websites that collect any personal data, use analytics or show ads need one, and many platforms and payment providers require it.

Can I edit the generated text?

Yes. Copy it into your editor and adjust it to match exactly how your business works.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.
