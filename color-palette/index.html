Guide

How to use it

Pick a base color and a harmony rule, or hit Random. Click any swatch to copy its hex code, or copy the whole palette as CSS variables.

Steps

  1. Choose a base color, such as your logo color.
  2. Pick a harmony rule or click Random.
  3. Click swatches to copy hex codes, or copy the CSS variables.

Frequently asked questions

Is the color palette generator free?

Yes. The color palette generator is 100% free with no limits, no watermark and no paid tier.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no account, sign-up or email required. Open the page and start using it.

Can I export or download my results?

Yes. Use Save results in the Free forever box to download your inputs and results as PDF, Excel (.xlsx) or CSV, or tap Send to phone to share them as a plain-text note (share sheet, copy, .txt file or QR code). Files are created in your browser and include the date generated.

Which harmony should I choose?

Analogous palettes feel calm and cohesive; complementary palettes create contrast; triadic palettes feel vibrant and balanced.

How do I keep text readable on these colors?

Each swatch label switches between dark and light text for legibility. For body text, aim for a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.

Can I use these colors commercially?

Yes. Colors are not owned; use the palette anywhere.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.

Private by design: everything runs in your browser. Your files and text never leave your device.
