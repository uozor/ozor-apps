    Guide

    How to use it

    Select one or more images, pick the target format, and download each converted file. Transparent areas get your chosen background color when converting to JPG.

    Steps

    1. Select one or more images.
    2. Pick the target format: PNG, JPG or WEBP.
    3. Click Convert and download each file.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the image converter free?

    Yes. The image converter is 100% free with no limits, no watermark and no paid tier.

    Do I need an account or sign-up?

    No. There is no account, sign-up or email required. Open the page and start using it.

    Which format should I choose?

    JPG for photos, PNG when you need transparency or crisp edges, and WEBP for the smallest files on modern websites.

    What happens to transparent areas in JPG?

    JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with the background color you choose.

    Can I convert HEIC photos from my iPhone?

    Only if your browser can open HEIC files. Safari usually can; other browsers may not. On iPhone you can also set the camera to “Most Compatible” to save JPGs.

    Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

    No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.
