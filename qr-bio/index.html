How to use it

  1. Type the link or text you want the QR code to open.
  2. Download the QR code as a PNG for print or screens.
  3. Optionally build a link-in-bio page and download bio.html to host anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No. The QR code encodes your link directly, so it works as long as the link itself works. There is no redirect service in between.

How do I host the link-in-bio page?

Upload bio.html to any static host, such as GitHub Pages, Netlify or your own website, and link to it from your social profiles.

What size should I print a QR code?

At least 2 × 2 cm (about 0.8 inches) for close scanning. Go larger for posters, and always test with a phone first.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.
