Privacy Policy

How GWB handles information, cookies, analytics and advertising.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Summary

GWB's tools run in your browser. Files, text and numbers you use in a tool are processed on your device and are not uploaded to or stored by GWB. This policy explains the limited information that may be collected when you visit the site.

Information processed by the tools

Content you open or type into a tool stays on your device. Some tools remember small preferences in your browser's local or session storage (for example, whether you have seen the “Find a tool” helper and your last helper search). You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses in server logs for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Analytics

We may use a privacy-friendly analytics service (such as GoatCounter or Cloudflare Web Analytics) to count page views and understand which tools are useful. These services collect aggregated, non-identifying information like pages visited, referrer, browser type and country, and do not use cookies to track you across sites.

Advertising and cookies

We may display ads from third-party vendors, including Google. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (My Ad Center). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be asked for consent through a Google-certified consent management platform before personalized ads or related cookies are used.

Payments

Tips and supporter memberships are processed by Stripe. GWB does not receive or store your card details. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and contact

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. For any privacy question or request, email ozor.inc@gmail.com.
