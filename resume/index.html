Guide

How to use it

Paste a plain duty (e.g. 'handled customer emails'), pick a few details, and get several polished bullet variants built on the action + result + metric formula.

Steps

  1. Paste a plain job duty, such as “handled customer emails”.
  2. Choose a verb style and add a number and a result if you have them.
  3. Copy the bullet that fits best and adjust the details.

Frequently asked questions

Is the resume bullet improver free?

Yes. The resume bullet improver is 100% free with no limits, no watermark and no paid tier.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no account, sign-up or email required. Open the page and start using it.

Can I export or download my results?

Yes. Use Save results in the Free forever box to download your inputs and results as PDF or Excel (.xlsx), or tap Send to phone to share them as a plain-text note (share sheet, copy, .txt file or QR code). Files are created in your browser and include the date generated.

What makes a strong resume bullet?

A strong bullet starts with an action verb, says what you did, and shows a measurable result, such as volume, money saved or a percentage improvement.

Should I include numbers if I'm not sure of them?

Use honest estimates you can explain in an interview, such as “about 200 tickets a week”. Never invent results.

How many bullets should each job have?

Three to five focused bullets per role is typical. Put your most impressive result first.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.
