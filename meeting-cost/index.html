Guide

How to use it

Enter attendees and their average hourly rate (or annual salary), then start the live timer or estimate a planned meeting.

Steps

  1. Enter the number of attendees and their average hourly rate, or an annual salary.
  2. Set the planned length to see the estimated cost.
  3. Press Start during the meeting to watch the live cost.

Frequently asked questions

Is the meeting cost calculator free?

Yes. The meeting cost calculator is 100% free with no limits, no watermark and no paid tier.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no account, sign-up or email required. Open the page and start using it.

Can I export or download my results?

Yes. Use Save results in the Free forever box to download your inputs and results as PDF, Excel (.xlsx) or CSV, or tap Send to phone to share them as a plain-text note (share sheet, copy, .txt file or QR code). Files are created in your browser and include the date generated.

How is the meeting cost calculated?

Attendees × average hourly rate × meeting length. An annual salary is converted using 2,080 working hours per year.

Does it include overhead like benefits?

No. If you want a fuller picture, raise the hourly rate by about 25–40% to approximate benefits and overhead.

Why show the yearly cost?

A weekly meeting runs about 52 times a year, so small costs add up. The yearly figure helps decide whether a meeting is worth it.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.

Private by design: everything runs in your browser. Your files and text never leave your device.
