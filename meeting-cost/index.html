Guide

How to use it

  1. Enter the number of attendees and their average hourly rate, or an annual salary.
  2. Set the planned length to see the estimated cost.
  3. Press Start during the meeting to watch the live cost.

Frequently asked questions

How is the meeting cost calculated?

Attendees × average hourly rate × meeting length. An annual salary is converted using 2,080 working hours per year.

Does it include overhead like benefits?

No. If you want a fuller picture, raise the hourly rate by about 25–40% to approximate benefits and overhead.

Why show the yearly cost?

A weekly meeting runs about 52 times a year, so small costs add up. The yearly figure helps decide whether a meeting is worth it.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.
