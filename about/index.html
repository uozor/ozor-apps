About GWB

GWB: Greater World Business is a free toolkit operated by OZOR INC LLC, a US limited liability company.

Who operates GWB

GWB: Greater World Business is the public name of this site. It is operated by OZOR INC LLC, a United States limited liability company. The tools are for freelancers, small businesses, and creators who need a calculator, a document, or a file conversion without opening an account. Write to the operator at gwm.tech@greaterworldmedia.tech. We do not publish a street address here.

What we do

GWB builds simple, single-purpose tools that help freelancers, small businesses and creators get everyday jobs done: making invoices, merging PDFs, compressing images, trimming ringtones, calculating margins and more. There are currently 20+ tools, and we add new ones based on what people ask for. Each tool page keeps the form at the top and a reference guide beside it, with the formula, a worked example, and the cases the tool does not cover.

Who builds and tests the tools

The same small team that publishes the site writes the tools, the reference notes beside them, and the checks that run before a change is merged. Pages are static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. There is no application server storing your documents. Before release we recheck internal links, run an accessibility scan that has to stay at zero violations, and compare calculator output with the worked examples in the guides. We do not invent customer reviews or staff biographies. A review appears on the home page only when someone wrote it and allowed us to publish it.

Client-side, zero-upload architecture

Every tool runs in your web browser. Files, text, and numbers are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to us. There is no account, no file-upload endpoint, and no database of the invoices, images, or tax figures you type. Closing the tab discards the work unless you downloaded it. A short list of recently opened tools, and whether you have opened the “Find a tool” helper, stay in this browser’s local storage and never leave the device. That zero-upload design is why the tools can stay free and why a PDF you merge here is not sitting on our disk afterward. Hosting logs, cookieless page counts, optional ads, and Stripe (only if you send a tip) are the exceptions, and they are spelled out on the privacy policy.

How 2026 tax and rate figures are checked

Money tools are planning estimates. They are not a tax return, a payroll filing, or legal advice. For tax year 2026, federal ordinary brackets and the standard deduction follow IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32, the inflation-adjustment procedure described in the IRS newsroom note on the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. The wage calculator uses a standard deduction of $16,100 single or married filing separately, $32,200 married filing jointly, and $24,150 head of household. Employee Social Security is 6.2 percent of wages up to the Social Security Administration’s 2026 wage base of $184,500. Medicare is 1.45 percent of all wages, plus the 0.9 percent Additional Medicare Tax above $200,000 single or head of household, $250,000 married filing jointly, or $125,000 married filing separately. The 401(k) field stops at the 2026 elective deferral limit of $24,500. State wage tables follow the Tax Foundation compilation of 2026 state individual income-tax rates, including each state’s standard deduction and any personal exemption or credit that table records. The sales-tax menu uses the Tax Foundation 2026 midyear statewide rates as of July 1, 2026, and it lets you type a combined local rate because a city is not a statewide average. We update the year when those published tables change, and every export is labeled as an estimate. Credits, itemized deductions, local wage taxes, and self-employment tax are left out of the wage calculator on purpose. Each calculator’s guide shows the formula and a number you can recompute by hand.

How the site is supported

The tools stay free. There is no paid tier and no account that unlocks a feature. Support is optional and labeled in the interface:

  • Tips and a supporter link. A one-time tip and a $3-a-month supporter checkout are Stripe payment pages. We never see your card number. Paying does not change the tool.
  • Partner recommendations. Some pages include a “Recommended tools” section. Those links are marked sponsored. If you buy, GWB may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The recommendation does not change a calculation or a file.
  • Sidebar ads. On a wide screen, Google AdSense units for publisher ca-pub-9780755121157050 sit in the side column, beside the tool rather than on top of it. On a small screen, one banner sits above the footer. Ads do not pop up, do not cover the words you are reading, and do not use an interstitial.
