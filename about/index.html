About GWB

GWB: Greater World Business is your one-stop shop for free small business tools.

What we do

GWB builds simple, single-purpose tools that help freelancers, small businesses and creators get everyday jobs done: making invoices, merging PDFs, compressing images, trimming ringtones, calculating margins and more. There are currently 20+ tools, and we add new ones based on what people ask for.

How our tools work

Every tool runs entirely in your web browser. Your files, text and numbers are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to us. That makes the tools fast, private and free to run, which is why we can offer them without accounts or limits.

How GWB is funded

The tools are free forever. We are supported by optional tips and supporter memberships, and we may show a small number of clearly labeled advertisements. We never use pop-ups, interstitials or ads that cover content.

Get in touch

Have an idea for a tool, found a bug, or want to share feedback? Visit our contact page or email gwm.tech@greaterworldmedia.tech.
