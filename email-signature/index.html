Frequently asked questions

Is the email signature generator free?

Yes. The email signature generator is 100% free with no limits, no watermark and no paid tier.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no account, sign-up or email required. Open the page and start using it.

Can I export or download my results?

Yes. Use Save results in the Free forever box to download your inputs and results as PDF or Excel (.xlsx), or tap Send to phone to share them as a plain-text note (share sheet, copy, .txt file or QR code). Files are created in your browser and include the date generated.

How do I add it to Gmail?

In Gmail, open Settings → See all settings → Signature, create a new signature, and paste.

How do I add it to Outlook?

In Outlook, go to Settings → Mail → Compose and reply (or Signatures), create a signature, and paste.

Why does my signature look different in some apps?

Email apps render HTML differently. This generator uses simple table-based inline styles for the best compatibility.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.
