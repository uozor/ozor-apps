Frequently asked questions

How do I add it to Gmail?

In Gmail, open Settings → See all settings → Signature, create a new signature, and paste.

How do I add it to Outlook?

In Outlook, go to Settings → Mail → Compose and reply (or Signatures), create a signature, and paste.

Why does my signature look different in some apps?

Email apps render HTML differently. This generator uses simple table-based inline styles for the best compatibility.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.
