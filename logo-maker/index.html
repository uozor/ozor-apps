Guide

How to use it

Type your business name, pick an icon, layout, font and colors, and watch the preview update. Download the logo as SVG or high-resolution PNG, or a favicon pack with favicon.ico, PNG sizes, an Apple touch icon and a web manifest.

Steps

  1. Type your business name and an optional tagline.
  2. Pick an icon, layout, font, icon shape and colors.
  3. Download the logo as SVG or PNG, or download the favicon pack for your website.

Frequently asked questions

Is the logo maker free?

Yes. The logo maker is 100% free with no limits, no watermark and no paid tier.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. There is no account, sign-up or email required. Open the page and start using it.

Can I export or download my results?

Yes. Use Save results in the Free forever box to download your inputs and results as PDF or CSV, or tap Send to phone to share them as a plain-text note (share sheet, copy, .txt file or QR code). Files are created in your browser and include the date generated.

Can I use the logo commercially?

Yes. The icons are simple original shapes made for this tool and the text is yours, so you can use the result for your business. Check that your business name does not infringe someone else's trademark.

What is in the favicon pack?

A ZIP with favicon.ico (16, 32 and 48 px), favicon-16x16.png, favicon-32x32.png, apple-touch-icon.png (180 px), android-chrome 192 and 512 px PNGs, site.webmanifest and a snippet of HTML tags to paste into your site's head.

Why does the font look different on another computer?

The logo uses common system fonts. For a pixel-perfect logo everywhere, use the PNG download, which bakes the text in as an image.

Are my files or text uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter or open is sent to our servers or stored by us.

Private by design: everything runs in your browser. Your files and text never leave your device.
